## Limitations and final-source closure

The Magan and Reminet origin worktrees had 7 and 168 local status entries at
setup. The fixtures were created exclusively from their resolved committed
HEAD archives; none of those uncommitted files entered the fixtures. The exact
counts and origin commits remain in setup metadata.

This document records the dogfood tranche only. Committing this evidence file
necessarily postdates the tested source commit. Final push readiness therefore
requires a clean rebuild at the resulting HEAD, byte identity with the tested
planner and Mise artifacts, the complete final-source gate suite, complete
authority-wide evidence verification, and a clean audit. Those confirmations
belong in the append-only release task result and final authority export rather
than as a self-referential claim inside this immutable dogfood receipt.
